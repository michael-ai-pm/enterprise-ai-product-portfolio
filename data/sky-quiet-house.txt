Sky Commissions The Quiet House, a Psychological Thriller from Blackfen Television Starring Idris Kane
Source: Sky Group Media Centre, Press Release
Date: 09 January 2026
URL: https://www.skygroup.sky/article/the-quiet-house-blackfen
Broadcaster: Sky Atlantic, NOW
Genre: Drama, Thriller
Production companies: Blackfen Television

Sky has commissioned The Quiet House, a six-part psychological thriller produced by Blackfen Television. The series follows a sound engineer who begins to hear voices in the recordings of an empty house he has been hired to survey, and whose investigation into their source unravels his grip on what is real.

Set across a single sweltering London summer, the drama plays with silence and noise as a character in its own right, using the protagonist's profession to blur the line between what is recorded and what is imagined.

The series marks a return to television for feature writer Delphine Moreau, whose screenplay was the subject of a competitive commissioning race.

The Quiet House is commissioned by Cameron Blythe, Sky Director of Drama. The Executive Producers are Delphine Moreau and Raj Patel for Blackfen Television. Filming takes place in London through 2026 for a 2027 transmission on Sky Atlantic and NOW.
