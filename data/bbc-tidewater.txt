BBC Commissions Tidewater, a Welsh-Language Drama from Aber Films for BBC and S4C
Source: BBC Media Centre, Press Release
Date: 02 June 2026
URL: https://www.bbc.co.uk/mediacentre/2026/tidewater
Broadcaster: BBC iPlayer, S4C
Genre: Drama, Thriller
Production companies: Aber Films

BBC and S4C have jointly commissioned Tidewater, a six-part Welsh-language thriller produced by Aber Films and set in a declining coastal town on the Ceredigion coast. The series follows a harbourmaster who discovers a body in a scuttled boat and the town's collective decision to look away.

Shot bilingually with a primary Welsh-language version, the drama is a slow-burn study of a community protecting itself, where everyone knows more than they say and the tide keeps returning what was meant to stay hidden.

The series continues the BBC's investment in indigenous-language drama with international festival ambitions.

Tidewater is commissioned by Lindsay Frost, BBC Head of Drama Commissioning, with Nia Gwilym for S4C. The Executive Producer is Dylan Prosser for Aber Films. It transmits in 2027 on S4C and BBC iPlayer.
