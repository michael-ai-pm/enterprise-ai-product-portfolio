Channel 4 Commissions The Reset, a Reality Format from Halcyon North Following People Who Quit Everything
Source: Channel 4 Press, Media Release
Date: 28 April 2026
URL: https://www.channel4.com/press/news/the-reset-halcyon-north
Broadcaster: Channel 4, Channel 4 Streaming
Genre: Entertainment, Reality
Production companies: Halcyon North

Channel 4 has commissioned The Reset, an eight-part reality format produced by Halcyon North that follows people who have walked away from their careers, homes and routines to start again from nothing in an unfamiliar part of the country.

Each participant is given a modest stake, a one-way ticket and ninety days. The series tracks whether a clean break delivers the reinvention they imagined, or whether the life they fled simply reassembles itself somewhere new.

With a documentary sensibility rather than a competitive one, the format taps a widely shared fantasy of starting over and tests it against reality.

The Reset is commissioned by Priya Anand, Channel 4 Commissioning Editor. The Executive Producer is Dominic Searle for Halcyon North. It airs on Channel 4 in 2027.
