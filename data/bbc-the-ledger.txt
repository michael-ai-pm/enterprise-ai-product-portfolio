BBC Commissions The Ledger, a Contemporary Crime Drama from Salt Bay Films Starring Chiwetel Osei
Source: BBC Media Centre, Press Release
Date: 11 June 2026
URL: https://www.bbc.co.uk/mediacentre/2026/the-ledger
Broadcaster: BBC One, BBC iPlayer
Genre: Drama, Crime
Production companies: Salt Bay Films

BBC One has commissioned The Ledger, a six-part contemporary crime drama produced by Salt Bay Films. The series follows a forensic accountant seconded to a police financial crimes unit, who discovers that the fraud she is tracing runs through the force that hired her.

Eschewing the usual violence of the genre, the drama finds its tension in spreadsheets, shell companies and the quiet complicity of respectable people. Its protagonist is an outsider whose only weapon is her refusal to stop reading the numbers.

The series is set in Birmingham and makes the city's financial district a character in its own right.

The Ledger is commissioned by Lindsay Frost, BBC Head of Drama Commissioning. The Executive Producers are Nadia Rahman and Peter Voss for Salt Bay Films. The writer is Joseph Adeyemi. Transmission is planned for 2027 on BBC One.
