BBC Commissions Marsh Kings, a Natural History Series from Fenland Wild Narrated by David Attenborough
Source: BBC Media Centre, Press Release
Date: 21 May 2026
URL: https://www.bbc.co.uk/mediacentre/2026/marsh-kings
Broadcaster: BBC Two, BBC iPlayer
Genre: Factual, Natural History
Production companies: Fenland Wild

BBC Two has commissioned Marsh Kings, a five-part natural history series exploring the wetland ecosystems of Europe, from the Danube Delta to the Norfolk Broads. Produced by Fenland Wild, the series is narrated by David Attenborough.

Using long-lens and drone photography across four years of filming, the series captures the hidden drama of wetlands, the most biodiverse and least filmed of Europe's habitats. Each episode follows a keystone species through a full seasonal cycle.

The series makes the case that marshes, long dismissed as wasteland, are among the most important carbon stores and wildlife refuges on the continent.

Marsh Kings is commissioned by Lindsay Frost, BBC Head of Factual Commissioning. The Executive Producer is Thomas Beaumont for Fenland Wild. The series transmits on BBC Two in 2027.
