Disney+ Commissions Star Cadets, a Young Adult Sci-Fi Series from Lumina Pictures as a UK Original
Source: Disney+ Press, Media Release
Date: 16 April 2026
URL: https://press.disneyplus.com/star-cadets-lumina
Broadcaster: Disney+
Genre: Drama, Science Fiction
Production companies: Lumina Pictures

Disney+ has commissioned Star Cadets, a ten-part young adult science fiction series produced by Lumina Pictures at studios outside Cardiff. The series follows a class of teenage recruits at a training academy orbiting a dying star, where the exams are real and failure is fatal.

Aimed at a family and teen audience, the series blends coming-of-age drama with large-scale space adventure, built around an ensemble of six young leads drawn from an open casting call across the UK and Ireland.

The commission is part of Disney+'s stated push into locally produced originals for the European market.

Star Cadets is commissioned by the Disney+ EMEA originals team led by Carla Espinosa. The Executive Producers are Tobias Frank and Meera Nair for Lumina Pictures. Filming begins in 2026 for a 2027 release on Disney+.
