Sky Commissions Frostline, a Factual Series from Meridian North on the Race to Map the Arctic
Source: Sky Group Media Centre, Press Release
Date: 24 March 2026
URL: https://www.skygroup.sky/article/frostline-meridian-north
Broadcaster: Sky Documentaries, NOW
Genre: Factual, Documentary
Production companies: Meridian North

Sky has commissioned Frostline, a three-part factual series produced by Meridian North examining the geopolitical race to map and claim the Arctic seabed as the ice retreats. The series combines expedition footage with access to the scientists, diplomats and naval officers shaping the region's future.

Filmed aboard research vessels and at remote listening stations, the series lays out how a warming Arctic has become the most contested new frontier in global politics, where shipping lanes, minerals and military advantage are all suddenly in play.

The series balances the science of a changing ocean with the harder story of the nations manoeuvring to own it.

Frostline is commissioned by Cameron Blythe, Sky Director of Factual. The Executive Producer is Anders Holt for Meridian North. It transmits on Sky Documentaries in 2027.
