Channel 5 Commissions Auction House Britain, a Factual Series from Gavel Media Following Regional Salerooms
Source: Channel 5 Press Office, Media Release
Date: 19 March 2026
URL: https://www.channel5.com/press/auction-house-britain-gavel
Broadcaster: Channel 5, 5
Genre: Factual, Observational
Production companies: Gavel Media

Channel 5 has commissioned Auction House Britain, a fifteen-part observational series produced by Gavel Media following the staff and sellers at three regional auction houses across a trading year. The series watches ordinary objects pass through the saleroom and the extraordinary stories attached to them.

From a house clearance that hides a forgotten master to a divorcing couple splitting a lifetime of possessions under the hammer, the series finds human drama in the everyday business of buying and selling.

Warm and gently addictive, the format is built for Channel 5's established daytime and early peak factual audience.

Auction House Britain is commissioned by Daniel Hart, Channel 5 Commissioning Editor for Factual. The Executive Producer is Bill Chandra for Gavel Media. It airs on Channel 5 from late 2026.
