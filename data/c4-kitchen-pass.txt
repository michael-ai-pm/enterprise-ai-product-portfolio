Channel 4 Commissions Kitchen Pass, a Competition Format from Copper Pot Productions Set in Regional Restaurants
Source: Channel 4 Press, Media Release
Date: 05 February 2026
URL: https://www.channel4.com/press/news/kitchen-pass-copper-pot
Broadcaster: Channel 4, Channel 4 Streaming
Genre: Entertainment, Competition
Production companies: Copper Pot Productions

Channel 4 has commissioned Kitchen Pass, a twelve-part competition series produced by Copper Pot Productions. The format sends amateur cooks to work a real service in a different regional restaurant each week, judged not by celebrity chefs but by the working kitchen brigades who have to rely on them.

The twist is accountability. Each contestant's dish goes out to real paying customers, and their survival depends on the verdict of the line cooks beside them rather than a panel at a distance.

Celebrating regional food culture away from the metropolitan restaurant scene, the series travels from a Bradford curry house to a Padstow seafood kitchen to a Glasgow bakery.

Kitchen Pass is commissioned by Priya Anand, Channel 4 Commissioning Editor. The Executive Producer is Louise Trent for Copper Pot Productions. It airs on Channel 4 in 2027.
