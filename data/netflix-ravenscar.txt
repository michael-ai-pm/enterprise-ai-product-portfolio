Netflix Commissions Ravenscar, a Fantasy Drama from Northlight Studios in Its First UK Original of 2026
Source: Netflix Media Centre, Press Release
Date: 30 March 2026
URL: https://about.netflix.com/en/news/ravenscar-northlight
Broadcaster: Netflix
Genre: Drama, Fantasy
Production companies: Northlight Studios

Netflix has commissioned Ravenscar, an eight-part fantasy drama produced by Northlight Studios and shot across Yorkshire and Iceland. The series is set in a fractured kingdom where memory can be traded as currency, and follows a young thief who steals the wrong memory and inherits a war.

Built on a original mythology rather than an existing property, the series blends large-scale worldbuilding with an intimate story about what a person becomes when their past can be bought and sold.

The commission represents one of Netflix's largest UK production investments of the year and confirms Northlight as a major domestic supplier of premium drama.

Ravenscar is commissioned by the Netflix UK originals team led by Owen Marsh. The Executive Producers are Beatrice Lund and Samuel Otto for Northlight Studios. The lead writer is Grace Ellery. Filming runs through 2026 and 2027.
