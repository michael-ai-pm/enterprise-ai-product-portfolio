Channel 4 Commissions Below the Line, a Documentary Series from Ironmarket Films on Britain's Gig Economy
Source: Channel 4 Press, Media Release
Date: 18 April 2026
URL: https://www.channel4.com/press/news/below-the-line-ironmarket
Broadcaster: Channel 4, Channel 4 Streaming
Genre: Factual, Documentary
Production companies: Ironmarket Films

Channel 4 has commissioned Below the Line, a four-part documentary series from Ironmarket Films following the lives of workers in Britain's gig economy across a single year. The series embeds with delivery riders, warehouse pickers and rideshare drivers in Manchester, Leeds and Coventry.

With unusual access to both workers and the platforms that employ them, the series examines how algorithmic management shapes daily working life, and what happens when a person's shifts, pay and standing are decided by software they cannot see or question.

The series avoids easy conclusions. It sits with the contradictions of flexible work that people value and precarity they cannot escape, and it does so through the eyes of the people living it.

Below the Line is commissioned by Priya Anand, Channel 4 Commissioning Editor for Documentaries. The Executive Producer is Mark Rennie for Ironmarket Films. The series director is Hana Osei. It airs on Channel 4 in early 2027.
