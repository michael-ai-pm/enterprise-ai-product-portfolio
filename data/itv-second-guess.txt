ITV Commissions Second Guess, a Daytime Quiz Show from Tenpin Television Hosted by Sandi Toksvig
Source: ITV Press Centre, Media Release
Date: 14 April 2026
URL: https://www.itv.com/presscentre/media-releases/second-guess-tenpin
Broadcaster: ITV1, ITVX
Genre: Entertainment, Quiz Show
Production companies: Tenpin Television

ITV has commissioned Second Guess, a daytime quiz show hosted by Sandi Toksvig. Produced by Tenpin Television, the format pits four contestants against the wisdom of a studio crowd, where the safest answer is rarely the most rewarding one.

In each round, contestants must decide whether to back their own knowledge or gamble on how the audience voted. Points swing wildly as the show rewards those who can read a room as well as recall a fact.

Pitched as a warm, cerebral addition to the afternoon schedule, the format leans on Toksvig's quickfire wit to keep the pace high across a sixty-episode run.

Second Guess is commissioned by Katie Rawcliffe, Director of Entertainment, Reality & Daytime Commissioning ITV. The Executive Producer is Gemma Sole for Tenpin Television. The series airs on ITV1 and ITVX from autumn 2026.
