ITV Commissions The Golden Elevators, a New High Stakes Quiz Show from Studio Lambert and Talpa Studios, Hosted by Richard Osman
Source: ITV Press Centre, Media Release
Date: 12 May 2026
URL: https://www.itv.com/presscentre/media-releases/itv-commissions-golden-elevators-new-high-stakes-quiz-show-studio-lambert-and-talpa
Broadcaster: ITV1, ITVX, STV, STV Player
Genre: Entertainment, Quiz Show
Production companies: Studio Lambert and Talpa Studios

ITV has commissioned The Golden Elevators, a high-stakes new quiz show hosted by Richard Osman. Produced by Studio Lambert and John de Mol's Talpa Studios, the series turns seemingly simple A or B questions into a game like no other, as ten contestants take on the game, each other, and two elevators in a bid to reach the penthouse and a huge cash jackpot.

Set in the lobby of a towering skyscraper, every question presents two possible answers, each represented by an elevator. Before the doors close, players can confer, debate, persuade and mislead. Then they choose. Step into the correct lift and move closer to the top. Step into the wrong one and everyone inside is plunged straight to the lobby and out of the game.

The jackpot is shared between every player who makes it to the penthouse. Will the group rise together through collective knowledge and trust, or will a lone wolf deceive their way to the top and take the whole cash prize for themselves? The series will air on ITV1, ITVX, STV and STV Player.

Richard Osman said he signed up the moment they showed him the game, calling it simple but requiring real skill to win, and unlike any quiz show he had seen.

Katie Rawcliffe, Director of Entertainment, Reality & Daytime Commissioning ITV, said the format is fast-paced, full of twists and turns, and play-along perfect for primetime.

The Golden Elevators is commissioned by Katie Rawcliffe, Director of Entertainment, Reality & Daytime Commissioning ITV, and Joe Mace, Entertainment & Reality Commissioning Editor ITV. The format is created and distributed by Talpa Studios. Executive Producers are Tom Blakeson, Tim Harcourt, Toni Ireland, Stephen Lovelock, Stephen Lambert, John de Mol and Jasper Hoogendoorn. Talpa reports advanced discussions with multiple territories, with ITV the first territory outside the Netherlands to take the format.
