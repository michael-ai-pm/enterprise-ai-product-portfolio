ITV Commissions Homefront, a Returning Drama from Kestrel Pictures Set on a Military Base
Source: ITV Press Centre, Media Release
Date: 08 May 2026
URL: https://www.itv.com/presscentre/media-releases/homefront-kestrel
Broadcaster: ITV1, ITVX
Genre: Drama, Contemporary
Production companies: Kestrel Pictures

ITV has commissioned Homefront, a six-part returning drama produced by Kestrel Pictures set on a British Army garrison. The series follows the families left behind during a long overseas deployment, and the parallel lives they build in the shadow of an absence that may or may not end.

Told entirely from the perspective of those who stay, the drama turns the usual war story inside out. The action is domestic, the stakes are marriages and mortgages and children growing up on video calls, and the enemy is uncertainty.

The series draws on extensive research with real forces families and is designed as a returning franchise across multiple deployments.

Homefront is commissioned by Polly Hill, ITV Head of Drama. The Executive Producers are Marcus Vane and Ellie Dunmore for Kestrel Pictures. The lead writer is Rachel Ngata. It films in 2026 for a 2027 launch on ITV1.
