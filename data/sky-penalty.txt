Sky Commissions Penalty, a Sports Entertainment Format from Crossbar Content Hosted by Gabby Logan
Source: Sky Group Media Centre, Press Release
Date: 12 March 2026
URL: https://www.skygroup.sky/article/penalty-crossbar
Broadcaster: Sky Max, NOW
Genre: Entertainment, Sport
Production companies: Crossbar Content

Sky has commissioned Penalty, a studio-based sports entertainment format produced by Crossbar Content and hosted by Gabby Logan. The show pits teams of former professionals and celebrity fans against each other in a mix of football knowledge rounds and physical challenges staged on a purpose-built indoor pitch.

Designed as a light-entertainment companion to Sky's live football, the format is fast, loud and built for clipping, with each episode producing a shareable moment engineered for social distribution.

The commission is part of Sky's push to extend its sports brand into formatted entertainment that lives beyond match days.

Penalty is commissioned by Cameron Blythe, Sky Director of Entertainment. The Executive Producer is Ryan Fielder for Crossbar Content. It launches on Sky Max in 2026.
