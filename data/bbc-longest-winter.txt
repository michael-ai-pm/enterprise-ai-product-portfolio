BBC Commissions The Longest Winter, a Six-Part Drama from Duncan Street Pictures, Starring Morven K9
Source: BBC Media Centre, Press Release
Date: 03 March 2026
URL: https://www.bbc.co.uk/mediacentre/2026/the-longest-winter
Broadcaster: BBC One, BBC iPlayer
Genre: Drama, Period
Production companies: Duncan Street Pictures

BBC One has commissioned The Longest Winter, a six-part period drama set in a remote Highland fishing village during the winter of 1919. Produced by Duncan Street Pictures, the series follows three families as the returning survivors of the Great War reshape a community that had already buried its dead.

The drama centres on Aisla Ferguson, a young widow running the village post office, who becomes the reluctant keeper of everyone's secrets as letters pass through her hands. When a stranger arrives claiming a dead soldier's inheritance, the fragile peace the village has built begins to fracture.

Shot on location across the west coast of Scotland, the series is a study of grief, resilience and the slow return of hope. The commission marks Duncan Street's first returning drama for the BBC.

The Longest Winter is commissioned by Lindsay Frost, BBC Head of Drama Commissioning. The Executive Producers are Callum Reid and Erin Whitfield for Duncan Street Pictures. The lead writer is Fiona MacAllister. Filming begins in autumn 2026 for transmission in 2027.
