Channel 5 Commissions Coastal Rescue, a Factual Series from Harbour Light Media Following Britain's Lifeboat Crews
Source: Channel 5 Press Office, Media Release
Date: 27 February 2026
URL: https://www.channel5.com/press/coastal-rescue-harbour-light
Broadcaster: Channel 5, 5
Genre: Factual, Observational
Production companies: Harbour Light Media

Channel 5 has commissioned Coastal Rescue, a ten-part observational series following volunteer lifeboat crews around the British coastline. Produced by Harbour Light Media, the series rides along with crews in Cornwall, Pembrokeshire and the north east of England through a full rescue season.

Each episode follows real callouts as they unfold, from stranded walkers cut off by the tide to vessels in distress miles offshore. Between the rescues, the series builds a portrait of the volunteers themselves, ordinary people who drop everything when the pager sounds.

The series is a warm, character-led celebration of a service that runs on unpaid courage, filmed with the cooperation of the crews and their families.

Coastal Rescue is commissioned by Daniel Hart, Channel 5 Commissioning Editor for Factual. The Executive Producer is Sarah Whitlock for Harbour Light Media. The series launches on Channel 5 in summer 2026.
